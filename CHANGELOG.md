### Performance

- debug-trace-server: thread resolved (number, hash) through the fetch pipeline, enforcing the pair on every serve tier ([#168](https://github.com/megaeth-labs/stateless-validator/pull/168))
- debug-trace-server: execute inbound JSON-RPC batch entries concurrently ([#176](https://github.com/megaeth-labs/stateless-validator/pull/176))
- debug-trace-server: negotiate gzip/zstd response compression at the origin ([#174](https://github.com/megaeth-labs/stateless-validator/pull/174))
